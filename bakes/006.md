## Notes

Rescue bake: salt went in early and the dough over-bulked after an accidental warm-oven proof, yet Carl still delivered strong spring, a bold ear, and a clean, set crumb. The short uncovered phase (~10 min at 425°F) gave the lighter, softer crust Jerrod prefers.

### Lessons

- **Salt:** weigh into a separate cup and add after the 45-min rest.
- **Never proof in an oven** someone else might preheat.
- **Temperature control:** Brød & Taylor Folding Proofer + Sourdough Home ordered (arriving Thu 2026-10-01).
