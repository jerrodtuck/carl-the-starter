# Boule bake day playbook

Baseline Carl boule for the Lodge Double Dutch. Full kitchen notes live in Obsidian; this is the public checklist.

## Locked formula

500 g bread flour · **340 g** water · 100 g peaked Carl · 10 g salt

Target dough temp after mix: **76–78°F** (calculate cooler mix water — don’t warm it).

## Day 1

1. Feed Carl (maintenance 1:1:1) so peak lands when you want to mix (~6.5 hr at ~74°F).
2. Mix flour + water + Carl. Rest 45 min. Add salt.
3. Into oiled 4 qt container. **Mark once. Never move the mark.**
4. Optional: early folds in first 2 hours, then hands off ≥60–90 min. (Bake 004: **0 folds**, shaped on volume — worked.)
5. Pull at settled **~50%** (~1.5 qt if start ~1 qt). Soft dome, living jiggle.
6. Pre-shape (gather + drag; wet scraper if sticky). Rest 20 min.
7. Final shape — if no folds, **create** the seam by gathering edges to center. Banneton rice flour, seam up.
8. Fridge 12–14 hr sweet spot (safe ~9–16). 35°F fridge is fine.

## Day 2

1. Lodge pieces into cold oven; preheat hard (aim ≥45–60 min at bake temp after the oven is truly hot).
2. Score cold: **box + T**.
3. Covered steam first (e.g. 450°F), then uncover / drop to 425°F for color.
   - **Lid-off color check at 10 min @ 425°F:** If crust is deep mahogany, foil tent or drop to 400°F. Color over clock.
4. Pull **205–210°F** internal **or** preferred Carl.4 color if darker. Cool **≥2 hours**. Weigh cooled loaf.

## Bake 004 callouts

- Dough temp **78.5°F** — on target after cooler water.
- Bulk 1550 → shape ~2130 at ~1.5 qt; retard **2155**; load ~**1105** next day.
- Oven not fully at 450 for most of lid-on — loaf still sprang and colored. Confirm dial before load next time.
