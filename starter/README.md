## Feed by cumulative weight (don’t tare between adds)

**Maintenance / single-loaf — 1:1:1 at 135%**

| Step | Stop at | Adds |
|------|---------|------|
| Spoon out down to | **357.3 g** | leaves 50 g seed |
| Add water to | **425.3 g** | 68 g water |
| Add rye to | **475.3 g** | 50 g rye |

Yields ~168 g: 100 g for dough, 50 g seed, ~18 g slack.

**Build before a double — 1:2:2 at 135%:** 50 g seed / 100 g rye / 135 g water (~285 g). Peaks later (~9.5 hr at ~74°F vs ~6.5 hr for 1:1:1).

## Method notes

Water before flour. Wet spoon and spatula; rinse in **cold** water after. At 135%, 100 g starter ≈ **42 g flour + 58 g water**.

## Why discard

Ratio of population to fresh food — not jar volume. Discard to a small base, feed a clean ratio, read the rise-and-fall curve.
