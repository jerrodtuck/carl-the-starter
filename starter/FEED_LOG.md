## Lessons so far

- **Fast culture.** Plan bulk short; watch the mark.
- Early “dead” readings were often **cold**, **too stiff to dome**, or **missed peak** between checks.
- Mix near peak. An hour or two past is fine; several hours past = slower bulk, more acid.
