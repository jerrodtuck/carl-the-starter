## Baseline boule (locked)

| Ingredient | Weight |
|------------|--------|
| Bread flour | 500 g |
| Water | **340 g** |
| Ripe Carl | 100 g |
| Salt | 10 g |

True hydration ~73% after Carl's water. Bake in a Lodge Double Dutch. Target dough temp after mix: **76–78°F**.

## House rules Carl taught us

1. He's **fast** — plan bulk short and watch the mark, not the clock.
2. Hit dough temp with calculated cooler water; don't warm the water "because recipes say so."
3. Pull bulk at settled **~50%** rise (~1.5 qt in a 4 qt Rubbermaid when dough starts near 1 qt).
4. Cool ≥2 hours before cutting.
