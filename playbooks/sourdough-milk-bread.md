# Sourdough milk bread (Zojirushi / discard)

Soft sandwich loaf. Instant yeast lifts; **Carl discard** adds tang. Leave peaked Carl for the boule.

**Preferred:** Zojirushi **Dough** cycle → shape into **USA Pan 9×4 Pullman** → oven 350°F to ~190°F internal.
**Lazy:** full Course 1 White in the machine.

## Enrichment

| Choice | Call |
|--------|------|
| Discard vs peaked | **Discard** (keep yeast) |
| Milk | 2% fine; whole is a small upgrade |
| Dry milk powder | **15–20 g** with flour — preferred on 2% |

## Full batch (~250 g discard → ~800 g dough)

Fits 9×4×4 Pullman (750–850 g range). ~2 maintenance discards ≈ 250 g.

| Ingredient | Weight |
|------------|--------|
| Milk (2%) | 93 g start (+ ~20 g aside if stiff) |
| Water | 28 g |
| Egg yolk | 1 (~18 g) |
| Butter, melted | 70 g |
| Sugar | 24 g |
| Discard | 250 g |
| Instant yeast | 6 g |
| Salt | 9 g |
| Bread flour | 300 g |
| Dry milk powder | 15–20 g |

Load: liquids first, dry (incl. powder), yeast last. Proof lid-on until ~1 in below rim.

### First bake (2026-09-15)

Scaled to 178 g discard, full machine Course 1 White, 2% milk, no powder. Soft sandwich crumb; great with picanha. Next: full batch + powder in the Pullman.
