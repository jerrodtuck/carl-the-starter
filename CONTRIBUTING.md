# How we log cooks

- **main** holds the settled chronicle (starter reference, finished bake notes, photos).
- **Each new cook starts on a branch**, e.g. `bake/005` or `cook/milk-bread-pullman`.
- While the bake is in progress, log draft notes and WIP photos on the branch.
- After crumb notes + photos are final, merge to `main` (PR preferred).

Carl does not ship half-baked lore to main.
